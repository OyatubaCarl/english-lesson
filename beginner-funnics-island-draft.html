入門編リライト案 - Funnics Island

既存の index.html は変更せず、入門編B1〜B20の文法ターゲットと、Funnics Islandの仲間たちを使った連続ストーリー案をここに保管します。

文法事項の全体像

1. be動詞の基礎

I am / You are、疑問文、否定文、This is / That is、He is / She is / It is。

2. 一般動詞の基礎

like / play / eat / have / go、Do you、don't、三単現の -s、Does / doesn't。

3. 疑問詞と名詞

what / who / when / where / how、複数形、主格・所有格・目的格。

4. 動きの表現

命令文、現在進行形、can / cannot、一般動詞の過去形と Did you。

ストーリー設計

舞台はFunnics Island。人間のTomが初めて島を訪れ、最初にTeacher Tacosに会います。Tomにとっては、しゃべるタコスの先生が島を案内し、パン屋のクマがパンを焼き、道や地図や道具まで反応する不思議な場所です。途中でTeacher Tacosがサルサの匂いを追っていなくなり、Aunt Antが代理ガイドになります。20レッスンを一本の長編に固定せず、島のいろいろな場所で起きる短い会話として作ります。

  1. B1〜B3: 島の入り口で、TomがTeacher Tacosに会い、「しゃべるタコスだ！」と驚く。Teacher Tacosがガイドになり、Aunt Antが島の友だちとして加わる。
  2. B4〜B6: 島の朝市で、好きなこと・すること・しないことを話す。
  3. B7〜B11: 地図、道具、仲間を紹介する。B9でTeacher Tacosがサルサを追って走り去り、Aunt Antが代理ガイドになる。
  4. B12〜B15: Aunt AntとTeacher Tacosを探し、B13で再会する。そのあと予定、場所、作り方、数えるものを使って、疑問詞と複数形を学ぶ。
  5. B16〜B20: ショー会場で、Juggler JellyfishとTeacher Tacosの出番を見ながら、命令・代名詞・現在進行形・can・過去形を使う。

差し替え英文案

Lesson B1 - Tom at the Gate

I am ... / You are ...
Tom, Teacher Tacos

(Teacher Tacos) Hello! Welcome to Funnics Island.

(Teacher Tacos) What's your name?

(Tom) Hi! My name is Tom.

(Teacher Tacos) Nice to meet you, Tom.

(Teacher Tacos) I am Teacher Tacos.

(Tom) Oh! A talking taco!

(Teacher Tacos) Correct!

(Teacher Tacos) I am the gate guide today.

(Teacher Tacos) You are welcome here.

(Tom) Wow! I am excited.

(Tom) This place is amazing.

(Teacher Tacos) Yes. This island is a little strange, too.

人間のTomが島の門に来て、最初にTeacher Tacosに会う。Tomが「しゃべるタコスだ！」と驚き、Teacher Tacosがガイド役になる。

Lesson B2 - Are You Ready?

Are you ...? / Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
Tom, Teacher Tacos

(Teacher Tacos) OK, Tom, are you ready?

(Tom) Hmm... Yes, I am.

(Teacher Tacos) Are you hungry?

(Tom) No, I am not.

(Tom) I am just curious. Wow.

(Teacher Tacos) Are you nervous?

(Tom) A little.

(Tom) Hmm... Are the island friends friendly?

(Teacher Tacos) Yes. Very friendly.

(Teacher Tacos) Don't worry.

(Tom) OK. Then I am ready.

島に入る前、Tomが少し緊張する。Teacher Tacosが「島の仲間はやさしい」と安心させる。

Lesson B3 - Not Alone

I am not ... / You are not ...
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Aunt Ant

(Tom) Teacher Tacos, I am not from this island.

(Teacher Tacos) I know, Tom. Don't worry.

(Teacher Tacos) You are not alone.

(Aunt Ant) Hello, Tom. I am Aunt Ant.

(Tom) Hi, Aunt Ant. You are not a taco!

(Aunt Ant) That's right! I am an ant.

(Teacher Tacos) This island is not only for tacos.

(Aunt Ant) You are not a taco, but you are welcome here.

(Tom) Wow! I am not alone. I have new friends here.

Tomが自分はこの島の出身ではないと伝える。Teacher TacosとAunt Antが「ひとりではない」「タコスではなくても歓迎される」と安心させる流れにする。

Lesson B4 - The Island Market

一般動詞
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Baker Bear, Cowboy Cat, Bread

(Teacher Tacos) Good morning, Tom. I like the island market.

(Tom) Wow, I like the colors.

(Baker Bear) Hi, Tom. I bake bread here.

(Tom) Yum. I eat bread at home.

(Bread) I wave at Tom.

(Tom) Wow! The bread waves!

(Cowboy Cat) Hi, Tom. I count cups for the juice stand.

(Tom) Whoa! The cups jump!

(Cowboy Cat) Yes. Cups jump on Funnics Island.

(Teacher Tacos) OK. You have a small map.

(Tom) OK. I follow the red path.

Tomが島の市場を見る。パンが手を振り、カップがぴょんと跳ねるなど、普通の市場ではない楽しさをはっきり入れる。

Lesson B5 - Not Alone

I am not ... / You are not ...
Tom, Teacher Tacos

(Tom) Hmm... Teacher Tacos, I am not from this island.

(Teacher Tacos) I know.

(Teacher Tacos) Don't worry.

(Teacher Tacos) You are not alone.

(Tom) Oh. I am not an island friend yet.

(Teacher Tacos) Not yet.

(Teacher Tacos) But don't worry.

(Teacher Tacos) You are my student today.

(Tom) Oh! Teacher Tacos is not just a name?

(Teacher Tacos) Yes, correct! I am not just a taco.

(Teacher Tacos) I teach, too.

(Tom) Wow! You are not just a talking taco.

(Tom) You are a real teacher.

B1で名前として聞いたTeacher Tacosが、実は本当に先生役でもあったとTomが気づく。しゃべるタコスの驚きは残しつつ、名前だけではない不思議さとガイド・生徒の関係を強める。

Lesson B6 - I Don't Know the Road

I don't ... / You don't ...
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Road, Runner Rabbit

(Tom) Teacher Tacos, I don't know the road.

(Teacher Tacos) Don't worry. You don't need a big map. The road talks.

(Road) Don't worry, Tom. I know the way.

(Tom) OK. I don't run on a new road.

(Runner Rabbit) Hi, Tom. I run every day.

(Teacher Tacos) Easy, Tom. You don't need speed today.

(Tom) Phew. I don't want a race. I want a slow walk.

(Runner Rabbit) A slow walk? So strange!

(Tom) Oh! The road is talking, so I walk slowly.

知らない道に戸惑うTomに、道そのものが声をかける。何でも走りたがるRunner Rabbitとの対比で会話に動きを出す。

Lesson B7 - This Map, That Gate

This is ... / That is ...
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Engineer Egg, Map, Pilot Panda

(Engineer Egg) Hi, Tom. Look! This is a map.

(Map) Hey! This is me!

(Tom) Aha! This is the market.

(Teacher Tacos) Yes. Good. That is the blue gate.

(Tom) Is that the sound hill?

(Engineer Egg) Yes, that is the sound hill.

(Pilot Panda) Hi, Tom. That is a boat by the gate.

(Tom) Wow. This is a big island.

(Teacher Tacos) And that is only the first page. Surprise!

(Tom) Wow! The map is talking, too.

地図を見ても島の全体がまだ見えないうえに、地図自身がしゃべる。島の道具も生きているワクワクを入れる。

Lesson B8 - What Is This Machine?

What is ...? / What do you ...?
Tom, Engineer Egg, Sound Machine, Magician Mouse, Key Box

(Tom) Engineer Egg, what is this?

(Engineer Egg) Look. This is a sound machine.

(Sound Machine) Ding! I am awake. Hello!

(Tom) OK. What do you make with this machine?

(Engineer Egg) I make a bell sound. Listen.

(Magician Mouse) Hi, Engineer Egg. What do you need?

(Engineer Egg) Wait... I need a small key.

(Tom) Oh? What is that?

(Magician Mouse) Ta-da! That is the key box.

(Tom) What is in the box?

(Magician Mouse) A small surprise. Hee-hee.

(Key Box) Knock, knock!

(Tom) Whoa! The key box is talking!

音の機械が「起きたよ」と鳴り、鍵箱がノックする。箱や機械まで反応する島だとわかる。

Lesson B9 - Who Is That Singer?

Who is ...? / Who do you ...?
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Aunt Ant, Singer Snake, Old Book, Librarian Lion

(Tom) Teacher Tacos, who is that?

(Teacher Tacos) Look. That is Singer Snake.

(Singer Snake) Hi, Tom. I sing for the island.

(Teacher Tacos) Wait. I smell super salsa. Oh wow. I must follow that smell!

(Tom) Oh no! Teacher Tacos is running away!

(Aunt Ant) Hi, Tom. Don't worry. I can be your guide for now.

(Tom) Thank you, Aunt Ant. OK. Who do you help, Singer Snake?

(Singer Snake) I help Baker Bear and Tom. La-la!

(Old Book) Wait... Who is by me?

(Tom) Oh! The old book is talking!

(Librarian Lion) Hello? Who is by the old book?

(Tom) OK, I am by the old book. Who is on the show stage?

(Aunt Ant) Juggler Jellyfish is on the stage. Teacher Tacos is... maybe with the salsa.

Tomが歌い手を見つけた直後、Teacher Tacosが超サルサの匂いを追って走り去る。Aunt Antが一時的にガイドを引き受け、古い本まで「だれ？」と話す。

一時注釈: must は「どうしても〜しなきゃ」という勢いとして扱う。

Lesson B10 - He, She, It

He is ... / She is ... / It is ...
Tom, Aunt Ant, Doctor Dragon, Juggler Jellyfish, King Kangaroo

(Tom) Teacher Tacos is gone.

(Aunt Ant) Don't worry. I can show you friends.

(Tom) OK. Who is he?

(Aunt Ant) He is Doctor Dragon. He is kind. Great!

(Tom) And who is she?

(Aunt Ant) She is Juggler Jellyfish. She is a juggler. Wow!

(Tom) Whoa! What is that in the air?

(Juggler Jellyfish) It is a jumping cup. It loves drama.

(Tom) Who is he by the stage?

(Aunt Ant) He is King Kangaroo. He is the king. Careful!

(King Kangaroo) Hello, Tom! Wow! My crown is jumping, too.

Teacher Tacosがいない間、Aunt Antが案内役を引き受ける。Doctor DragonとKing Kangarooはhe、Juggler Jellyfishはshe、空中で跳ねるショー用カップはitで紹介する。

Lesson B11 - Baker Bear Bakes

三単現 / Does ...? / doesn't
Tom, Aunt Ant, Baker Bear, Cowboy Cat, Bread

(Aunt Ant) Look. Baker Bear bakes bread every morning.

(Bread) Good morning! Yay!

(Tom) Wow. He works early.

(Cowboy Cat) Yes. He likes warm bread.

(Tom) Does the bread say good morning every day?

(Baker Bear) Yes, it does. Every day.

(Tom) OK. Does he bake cakes, too?

(Cowboy Cat) Yes, he does. No. He doesn't bake fish cakes.

(Tom) Good! I like sweet cakes. Does Teacher Tacos teach every day?

(Cowboy Cat) Yes, he does. Wow. He teaches with salsa cards.

(Aunt Ant) And today he follows a salsa smell. Too much!

Aunt Antの案内でBaker Bearの日課を見る。毎朝パンがあいさつすること、Teacher Tacosがサルサカードで教えることも本人不在のまま話題にする。

Lesson B12 - When is ...? / When do you ...?

When is ...? / When do you ...?
Tom, Aunt Ant, Singer Snake, Clock, Juggler Jellyfish

(Tom) When is the show?

(Clock) Now? Now? Please? Start the show now!

(Tom) Whoa! The clock is too excited!

(Aunt Ant) OK, clock. The show is at two. The clock is always early.

(Tom) OK. When do you sing, Singer Snake?

(Singer Snake) I sing at one thirty. Easy.

(Tom) Great. When does Juggler Jellyfish start?

(Juggler Jellyfish) I start at two ten. Don't panic. My cups panic enough.

(Tom) When does Teacher Tacos teach?

(Aunt Ant) He teaches after the juggling. But today... he may be late.

(Tom) Oh no! The salsa smell is stronger.

Aunt Antの案内で、Teacher Tacos不在のままショーの時間を聞く。時計はせっかちすぎて「今すぐ始めて！」と急かし、Teacher Tacosはサルサのせいで遅れそうだとわかる。

Lesson B13 - Where is ...? / Where do you ...?

Where is ...? / Where do you ...?
Tom, Aunt Ant, Stage, Salsa Jar, Teacher Tacos

(Tom) Where is Teacher Tacos?

(Aunt Ant) I hear salsa drums by the big tree.

(Tom) OK. Where is the show stage?

(Stage) Um... I am here by the big tree. Please don't stare.

(Tom) Where does the salsa smell go?

(Salsa Jar) Over here! Too spicy! Too famous!

(Tom) Oh! A shouting salsa jar! Where is Teacher Tacos?

(Teacher Tacos) I am under the red table.

(Tom) Wow! Teacher Tacos is back!

(Teacher Tacos) I am your guide again.

(Tom) Great. Where do you stand?

(Teacher Tacos) I stand on the red mat.

TomがTeacher Tacosの居場所を探す。サルサの匂いと叫ぶサルサ瓶をたどり、赤いテーブルの下でTeacher Tacosと再会する。

Lesson B14 - How are you? / How do you ...?

How are you? / How do you ...?
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Engineer Egg, Sound Machine

(Teacher Tacos) Hi, Tom. How are you? OK?

(Tom) Wow! I am fine now. I feel safe because you are back.

(Teacher Tacos) Good. I am back, and I am your guide.

(Tom) How are you, Teacher Tacos?

(Teacher Tacos) I am spicy, but safe. The salsa jar is too much.

(Tom) The island is amazing. Engineer Egg, how do you make that sound?

(Engineer Egg) OK. I turn this key and tap this box.

(Sound Machine) Ding, dong! Oops! I am too loud!

(Tom) Teacher Tacos, how do you teach?

(Teacher Tacos) I point, I tap, and I say, "Ta-da!"

(Tom) Whoa! This island is wonderful. Even the machine sings too much.

Teacher Tacosが戻ってきてTomが安心する。そこから音の機械とTeacher Tacosの教え方を聞き、「どうやって？」が本当に知りたい質問になる。

Lesson B15 - 名詞の複数形

名詞の複数形
Tom, Queen Quiz, Cowboy Cat, Teacher Tacos, Shells, Flags

(Tom) Wow! I see many shells.

(Shells) Clap, clap, clap! Too much?

(Cowboy Cat) OK. I count shells every day.

(Queen Quiz) Hi, Tom. Ta-da! I have three flags.

(Tom) OK. I have two boxes.

(Teacher Tacos) Great. I have four salsa cards.

(Queen Quiz) Yes. The boxes are for the island quiz.

(Tom) Wow. The flags are bright.

(Flags) We dance too much!

(Cowboy Cat) And the cups are for Juggler Jellyfish.

ショーの道具を数える。貝は拍手しすぎ、旗は踊りすぎ。Teacher Tacosのsalsa cards、Juggler Jellyfishのcupsで出演者につなげる。

Lesson B16 - 命令文 / Don't ...

命令文 / Don't ...
Tom, Guardian Goat, Teacher Tacos, Bridge

(Guardian Goat) Hi, Tom. OK, open the small gate, please.

(Teacher Tacos) Easy. Walk slowly.

(Guardian Goat) Careful! Don't run on the bridge.

(Bridge) Walk, please. Hee-hee! I giggle too much.

(Tom) Yikes! The bridge is too ticklish!

(Teacher Tacos) OK. Put the boxes on the table. Listen to me.

(Teacher Tacos) Touch the salsa cards, but don't eat the teacher.

(Tom) Phew. I understand. Don't eat the teacher. OK. I am careful.

ショー会場に入る前の安全案内。橋はくすぐったがりすぎて、歩かれるだけで笑いすぎる。「先生タコスを食べないで」という島ならではの注意で面白くする。

Lesson B17 - 主格・所有格・目的格

主格・所有格・目的格
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Librarian Lion, Old Book, Salsa Cards

(Tom) OK, this is my map.

(Teacher Tacos) Yes, good. And this is your island pass.

(Old Book) Hey! Tom, read me now!

(Librarian Lion) Here. I have your book, Tom.

(Tom) Please give it to me.

(Teacher Tacos) Oh! My salsa cards are on the stage.

(Salsa Cards) Hey! Give us to Teacher Tacos now!

(Tom) OK. I give them to you.

(Teacher Tacos) Thank you. Great job. This cake is for you.

地図・入島パス・本・Teacher Tacosのカードを受け渡す。本とカードは主張が強すぎて急かしてくる。my / your / me / them を使う。

Lesson B18 - be動詞 + 動詞ing

be動詞 + 動詞ing
Tom, Juggler Jellyfish, Teacher Tacos, Cups

(Tom) Wow! The show is starting.

(Juggler Jellyfish) OK! I am juggling cups.

(Cups) Whoa! We are flying too fast!

(Tom) Whoa! The cups are flying too fast!

(Teacher Tacos) Now I am teaching the taco dance.

(Tom) Aha! Teacher Tacos is teaching a dance.

(Teacher Tacos) Yes. Wow. You are watching Funnics Island.

(Juggler Jellyfish) Oops! I am juggling and jumping too much.

(Teacher Tacos) OK. I am tapping the salsa cards.

ショーの出演者はJuggler JellyfishとTeacher Tacos。カップは速すぎ、Juggler Jellyfishは動きすぎ、Teacher Tacosがダンスを教える場面にする。

Lesson B19 - can / cannot / Can you ...?

can / cannot / Can you ...?
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Doctor Dragon, Juggler Jellyfish, Box

(Tom) Teacher Tacos, can I help?

(Teacher Tacos) Yes, you can. Great!

(Doctor Dragon) Hi, Tom. Please, can you carry this box?

(Box) Um... I can sing, but I cannot walk. I am too shy.

(Tom) Oh! The box can sing, but it hides! OK. Yes, I can.

(Teacher Tacos) Great. Can you hold my salsa cards?

(Tom) OK. Yes, I can.

(Juggler Jellyfish) Oh no! I cannot hold eight cups now.

(Tom) I can hold two cups. Easy!

(Teacher Tacos) Great! We can finish together.

ショーの裏側でTomが手伝う。箱は歌えるけれど恥ずかしがり屋すぎて歩けない。Teacher TacosのカードやJuggler Jellyfishのカップでcanを使う。

Lesson B20 - 過去形 / Did you ...?

過去形 / Did you ...?
Tom, Teacher Tacos, Juggler Jellyfish, All Friends

(Tom) I visited Funnics Island yesterday.

(Teacher Tacos) Yes. You walked around the market.

(Juggler Jellyfish) I juggled cups for you. Ta-da!

(Teacher Tacos) And I taught the taco dance.

(Tom) I saw an excited clock, a shy stage, and Teacher Tacos.

(Tom) I watched cups fly too fast on stage.

(Teacher Tacos) So, did you enjoy the island?

(Tom) Yes, I did. I learned English in a strange, wonderful place.

(All Friends) Good-bye, Tom! Come again!

最後はTomが「せっかちすぎる時計、恥ずかしがり屋すぎる舞台、Teacher Tacos、速すぎるカップ」をふり返る。島の不思議さを最後まで残す。
