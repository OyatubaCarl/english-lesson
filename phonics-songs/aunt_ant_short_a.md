# 🎵 Aunt Ant's Short A

短母音 /æ/ を扱うナンセンス・フォニックスソング。
**4 つの基本文 × 17 単語**だけで構成、組み合わせと属性まとめで反復学習。

## 🎵 Suno スタイル指示

```
Children's phonics song with bilingual Japanese-English structure.
Spoken Japanese intro and outro (about 10 seconds each, friendly female
narration). Main song: light upbeat nursery-rhyme melody, 100-110 BPM,
acoustic guitar + claps + finger snaps. Female lead vocals with kids
choir on the "A!" hooks. Each verse uses the same melody so kids notice
the substitution. About 1:50-2:10 total.

Pronunciation: chorus "A!" should sound like the letter name /eɪ/ (Ay),
the spoken "ショート A" preserves the Japanese tone, and verse words use
natural English with clear /æ/ emphasis.
```
