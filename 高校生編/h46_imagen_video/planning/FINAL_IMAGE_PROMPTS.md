# H46 final ImageGen direction

Built-in Codex ImageGen was used in image-generation mode. All images were generated at 16:9 with no embedded text, logos, watermarks, or infographics.

## Shared visual language

- Contemporary Japanese animated-feature look with detailed, luminous backgrounds and cinematic composition.
- Crisp character drawing, natural anatomy, subtle facial expressions, realistic fabrics and architecture.
- Blue and amber light may strengthen outdoor scenes, but lighting must remain motivated by the actual location.
- Indoor scenes use ordinary interior light and believable rooms; no forced sunset, dramatic sky, or ornamental fantasy scenery through windows.
- Diverse citizens, nonpartisan civic imagery, and no identifiable real politicians or party branding.

## Final scene prompts

1. **Ancient Athens** — A wide civic scene in the Athenian agora, citizens actively debating policy in small groups, marble stoas and the Acropolis in the distance, clear Mediterranean air, late-afternoon light, expansive but historically grounded.
2. **Constitution** — A quiet modern civic consultation room where a diverse high-school-age group and adults study a constitution together, warm wood and frosted windows, normal indoor daylight, focused expressions, document as the visual center.
3. **Election day** — An ordinary municipal polling room with ballot boxes, privacy booths, election staff, and citizens voting, neutral fluorescent/daylight mix, calm and procedural, no campaign material.
4. **Candidates' forum** — A modest community auditorium with several fictional candidates presenting policies to seated voters, simple wooden stage, normal indoor light, neutral signage without readable text.
5. **Parliamentary committee** — A practical committee room with elected representatives reviewing a bill around a table, papers and microphones, restrained architecture and clerestory windows, serious collaborative discussion.
6. **Civic participation** — Citizens of different ages taking part in a neighborhood meeting and volunteer activity in an open public space, clear actions and hopeful daylight, participation rather than spectacle.
7. **Majority and minority** — Two different-sized groups meeting across a pedestrian bridge, listening to one another and exchanging documents, visual balance and mutual respect, river and city in blue-hour light.
8. **Shared future** — A broad final view of diverse citizens walking toward a civic plaza and open horizon, dignified optimism, luminous sky, subtle blue-and-gold palette, democratic society represented through shared movement.

The earlier picture-book images and the overly scenic indoor trials are retained only as comparison material and are not used in the final video.
