## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
Mr. Tanaka is seventy-two.
He retired five years ago from the company where he had worked for long years.
After his retirement, his days have passed slowly.

[Verse 2]
Early each morning, he goes out to the garden and waters the flowers his late wife had grown.
His wife passed away three years ago from a sudden illness.
He still remembers the day of the funeral vividly.

[Verse 3]
Every Sunday, he visits his wife's grave.
Before the stone, he joins his hands and quietly prays.
His silent prayer is carried away by the wind.
Mr. Tanaka carries many regrets.

[Bridge]
In his younger years, he had been consumed by work and had spent little time with his family.
His wife never complained, but in her late years she had whispered, I wish we had traveled more together.

[Verse 4]
Those words still linger in his heart.
Now, Mr.
Tanaka travels alone.
One by one, he visits the places his wife had wanted to see.
Somehow, he feels as if he is walking together with her.
The passage of life always comes quietly.

[Outro]
From youth to middle age, from middle age to old age.
And then, to farewell.
Mr. Tanaka, within the gently flowing time, spends his remaining days.
I want to finish the last chapter of my life quietly, he says.
```
