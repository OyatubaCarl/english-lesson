## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
As individual human beings, each of us is very small.
The Earth lies in a corner of a galaxy, and the universe holds hundreds of billions of galaxies.
When we imagine that scale, human activity seems insignificant.

[Verse 2]
Yet at the same time, is it not astonishing that we can have consciousness and wonder about the universe?
Ancient philosophers looked up at the night sky and thought.
Why are we here?

[Verse 3]
Is there meaning for us, who are only one aspect of all this?
We too, under the night sky, can hold the same questions.
Modern science teaches us much.
The universe originated 13.8 billion years ago.
Stars generate energy by fusion.

[Bridge]
Life arose from chemical reactions.
But why does being exist, and why is there this universe rather than nothing?
Science has no definitive answer to this root question.
Human consciousness is also mysterious.

[Verse 4]
Why, from the electrical signals of neurons, do the sensation of red or the emotion of joy emerge?
Neither psychologists nor philosophers has yet given a clear answer.
To face these questions comes with a painful loneliness.

[Outro]
But it is also a privilege given only to human beings.
Though we are small, we can think about the universe itself.
Is there anything more extraordinary than this?
```
