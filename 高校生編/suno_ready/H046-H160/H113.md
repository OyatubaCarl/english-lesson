## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
Modern society is full of contradictions.
A person who is materially prosperous may still feel unhappy inside.
Why?
With the spread of social media, we have come to relate to others more than ever before.
But at the same time, chances to compare ourselves with others have also increased.

[Verse 2]
The lives of others look attractive.
Beautiful travel destinations, delicious meals, impressive achievements.
Inevitably, our own life comes to feel dull.
But what social media shows is only a portion of another's life, and at that, a deliberately chosen moment — the most glittering one.

[Verse 3]
Everyone, behind the camera, hides the fatigue and worries of daily life.
Psychologists indicate that excessive social media use can become the basis for depression and anxiety.
Especially for the young, especially for teenagers, the consequences are serious.
The answer is not to quit social media entirely.

[Bridge]
It is to keep our distance and turn our eyes to our own life.
To step away from the folly of comparing another's peak with our own average.
True happiness is not found in the comparison with others, but in a sense of self-acceptance.

[Outro]
To indeed realize this is the challenge of those of us who live in the modern age.
```
