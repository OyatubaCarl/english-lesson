## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
Correspondents reporting from war zones play a vital role in informing us of what is happening in the world.
Journalists who go to conflict areas stake their own lives to convey the truth.
They are constantly exposed to danger.

[Verse 2]
Via satellite phones and the internet, their images and words are transmitted around the world instantly.
From our living room, we witness a distant battlefield in a visible form.
But the truth of war is not only what is shown on camera.

[Verse 3]
Much pain is invisible and cannot be conveyed on screen.
The sadness of refugees who have lost their homes, the suffering of children deprived of safety and education.
Among journalists, some sadly lose their lives on the ground.

[Bridge]
In the war in Ukraine since 2022 alone, many reporters have been killed.
They have become victims of murder, accidents, and attacks.
Even so, they continue their reporting.
Why?
Because they carry the conviction that if the truth is not told to the world, the same tragedy will happen again.

[Outro]
To protect the freedom of the press is to protect democracy.
And to the journalists who continue to report the truth at great risk, we cannot help feeling we owe something.
```
