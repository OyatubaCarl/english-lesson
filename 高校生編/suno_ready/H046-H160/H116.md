## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
My grandfather was a man who hardly spoke.
He seldom talked about himself, and rarely showed his emotions openly.
When I was young, that confused me.
What was he thinking?
Did he dislike me?

[Verse 2]
But as the years passed, I came to understand him.
His silence was not coldness, but a quiet love.
The generation that lived through the war tends to avoid talking about it.
Remembering itself causes pain.
They touch the past only briefly.

[Verse 3]
After my grandfather passed away, I found notebooks he had been writing.
In them, his detailed memories of the battlefield, and his private feelings about his family, were recorded in small handwriting.
He had truly loved us.

[Bridge]
It was simply difficult for him to put it into words.
For him, love was shown through action.
Tilling the field with me on his day off, secretly sending a small gift on his grandchild's birthday.

[Verse 4]
Inside my grandfather's silence, I grasped true kindness.
His silence did not mean that words were useless — rather, there was a love that went beyond words.
On my late grandfather's birthday, I go to his grave to offer flowers.

[Outro]
I briefly join my hands in prayer and quietly speak to him for a short time.
My grandfather, even now, in an invisible form, lives within my heart.
```
