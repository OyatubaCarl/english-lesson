Other countries are gradually strengthening restrictions.

[Verse 4]
Buy honey from local beekeepers.
Try gardening without pesticides.
Bees function within the network of the ecosystem.
If they disappear, many organisms will be affected throughout the food chain.

[Outro]
A small being supports a great world.
To protect bees is to protect our own future.
