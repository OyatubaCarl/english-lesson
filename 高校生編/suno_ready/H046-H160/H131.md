## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
My father, who was born and raised in a fishing village, has lived with the sea.
He would set out before dawn and judge the changing weather with care and support the family.
When my father was young, fish were abundant.

[Verse 2]
In the morning fishing, the nets would fill with silver fish.
Dealers waited at the port, and prices were set by auction.
But over the decades, the situation changed greatly.
The amount of fish in the sea kept decreasing.
The causes are complex.

[Verse 3]
Overfishing, ocean pollution, climate change.
At first, my father insisted that the old methods are enough.
His desire to preserve the conventional way of fishing was strong.
But changes in the times cannot be resisted.

[Bridge]
I, his son, introduced my father to new technology — fish finders and medium-sized seine nets.
My father, at first, resisted this mechanization.
But once he tried it, he was surprised at the efficiency.
Fishing methods must evolve, he came to say with a laugh.

[Verse 4]
But efficiency alone is not sustainable.
At the same time, we established closed fishing zones and made sure not to take too much.
Based on relevant scientific evidence, we monitor the fish population and aim for sustainable fishing.

[Outro]
My father now passes his experience on to younger fishermen.
So that the bounty of the sea reaches the generation of our children and grandchildren.
That is the shared wish of my father and me.
```
