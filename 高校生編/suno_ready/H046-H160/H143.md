## Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
In my old home stood an ancient piano.
It had been used by my grandmother in her youth and had the dignity of an antique.
The keys were yellowed, and the tuning was slightly off, but it was our family's treasure.

[Verse 2]
Every week my mother would gently wipe the piano, and once a year she called a tuner for its maintenance.
Music is a precious heritage of our family, she said.
From the age of six, I began lessons on this piano.

[Verse 3]
The first few years were bitter.
Practice seemed silly to me.
Hearing friends playing outside, I kept repeating scales.
But in middle school, piano gradually became enjoyable.

[Bridge]
I could play Chopin and Debussy and came to know the joy of expressing my emotions through sound.
At university I did not select music as my major.
But I continued playing the piano.

[Verse 4]
Even on busy days, the time at the keyboard was my spiritual rest.
Employment, marriage, raising children.
At every stage of life, the piano was with me.
On tired nights, sad days, joyful days.
Music absorbed my feelings and healed me.

[Outro]
My grandmother passed away, my mother passed away, and now it is my daughter who plays the piano.
My seven-year-old daughter lacks the strength in her fingertips to play Chopin.
But the sight of her stretching her toes toward the pedal recalls my own image long ago.
A single old piano has connected the family across four generations.
An instrument is not a mere object.
Like a chamber of family history and affection, it contains all the memories.
```
