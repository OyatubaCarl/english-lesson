[Verse 1]
Living abroad, one sees both the good and the bad of one's own country.
At thirty, I was posted to the United States for three years for work.
The first months brought surprise from cultural differences.

Different kept appearing.
Americans are direct.
They do not try to hide their opinions or emotions.
If they disagree, they argue clearly, openly.

But I also saw the dark side.
Gun crime, racial discrimination, the high cost of medical care.

Returning to Japan, I realized anew: safety, cleanliness, the precision of time.
In Japan, you can see a person walking alone at a station late at night.
You would not see this in America.

[Verse 4]
But Japan has its weaknesses too.
Conformity pressure, overwork, high suicide rates.
The individual is weak, and people, often pay too much attention to the eyes of others.
The experience of another culture is a mirror.

[Outro]
While you come to know the other country, you see your own country more clearly.
Experience abroad is made up of individual encounters.
Together, they have shaped me as a person.
