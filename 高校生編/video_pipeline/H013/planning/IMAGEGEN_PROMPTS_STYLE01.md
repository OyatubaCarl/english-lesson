# H013 ImageGen prompt set — style 01

## Approved style and priority

- Style: 01「光彩・劇場アニメ背景」
- Priority: lyric/setting consistency > character/location/time continuity > caption readability > beauty
- Format: 16:9, detailed hand-painted theatrical-anime illustration, clearly illustrated rather than photorealistic
- Camera: stable eye-level view; no Dutch angle, shake, motion blur, or exaggerated wide-angle distortion
- Text: no lyrics, captions, readable signs, logos, borders, or watermark in source images

## Continuity anchors

- Location: one modest, one-story suburban Japanese ground-level station and its residential neighborhood
- Time/weather: post-rain cloudy late afternoon, clearing only in the final outdoor scene
- Protagonist: 17-year-old Japanese male, short slightly tousled black hair, navy school jacket, white shirt, gray trousers, black backpack
- Friend: 17-year-old Japanese male, short chestnut hair, beige jacket, dark trousers, small dark shoulder bag
- Train: one consistent green commuter train
- Interior rule: opaque ceiling, fluorescent fixtures, structural walls and windows; sky only through real exterior windows or openings
- Forbidden: impossible indoor sky, fantasy panorama, grand terminal, resort setting, unexplained location/time changes

## Scene prompts

1. `01_station_run.png`: Same station exterior after rain. The protagonist runs toward the entrance while the green train begins to leave. Cloudy late afternoon, wet pavement, ordinary forecourt.
2. `02_train_departed.png`: Same protagonist safely on the wet platform, looking at the rear of the green train receding with red tail lights. The train is unmistakably departing, not approaching.
3. `03_waiting_room_empty_platform.png`: Same protagonist exhausted on a simple bench inside a normal waiting room. Opaque ceiling, fluorescent light, walls and frames. Through windows/door show only the empty wet platform and tracks; absolutely no train.
4. `04_road_detour_flashback.png`: Same protagonist running through a believable residential street blocked by fenced roadwork, with a visible usable side detour. Same rain and time conditions.
5. `05_friend_boarded_earlier.png`: A few minutes earlier, the distinct chestnut-haired friend steps across the open doorway threshold into the stopped green train. Protagonist absent.
6. `06_staff_explains.png`: Interior ticket-gate area. The same protagonist listens to one uniformed station employee gesturing toward the already-empty platform. Small secondary crowd; no train and no friend.
7. `07_phone_call.png`: Same waiting area. The protagonist holds a phone to his ear and begins to look relieved. Empty wet platform outside; friend is only on the phone and must not appear physically.
8. `08_waiting_sky_clears.png`: Outside the same station, protagonist alone on a small forecourt bench. Clouds part naturally into restrained late-afternoon brightness. Friend has not arrived yet.

## Rejection record

- The first waiting-room candidate was rejected because a green train remained visible outside after the lyric had established that it had already departed.
- The accepted replacement is `03_waiting_room_empty_platform.png` and shows an empty platform only.
