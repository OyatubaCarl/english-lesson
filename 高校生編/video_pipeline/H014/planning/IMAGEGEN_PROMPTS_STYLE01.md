# H014 ImageGen prompt set — style 01

## Approved style and production intent

- Use case: illustration-story
- Video: H014「あと5年後の自分」採用Suno曲の映像化
- Style: 01「光彩・劇場アニメ背景」
- Music mood: spacious chamber-pop, felt piano, cello, subtle ambient texture, minimal percussion; reflective and quietly hopeful
- Priority: lyric/time consistency > same-person continuity > natural rooms > caption readability > beauty
- Format: 16:9 cinematic 2D theatrical-anime illustration, precise drawn background, hand-painted cloud light, cel-painted characters, subtle atmospheric perspective
- Camera: stable eye-level view; no Dutch angle, shake, motion blur, fisheye, or exaggerated wide-angle distortion
- Caption safety: keep the primary face and key action mainly in the upper and middle half; the lower portion will receive a dark subtitle panel
- Prohibited: photorealism, photographic texture, PBR, 3D render, readable text, letters, numbers, logos, captions, borders, watermark

## Continuity anchors

- Present protagonist: 17-year-old Japanese male high-school student, short slightly tousled black hair, calm brown eyes, slim natural build, navy school jacket, white shirt, gray trousers, black backpack
- Future protagonist: the exact same person at age 22, same recognizable face/eyes/hair, slightly more mature, navy casual jacket over a gray knit, dark trousers, small brown messenger bag
- Story scale: modest neighborhood contribution and small achievements, never wealth, celebrity, executive success, trophy spectacle, or futuristic technology
- Color arc: quiet blue-gray evening in the present; restrained teal and amber in imagined future; gentle gold only in the final morning

## Scene prompts

1. `01_present_reflection.png`: A normal Japanese high-school bedroom in quiet blue-gray early evening. The present 17-year-old sits at a compact desk beside a real window, looking past a closed unmarked notebook toward the distant neighborhood lights, thoughtfully imagining his life five years from now. Real walls and ceiling, ordinary books and one small plant, no readable writing. Intimate wide composition, calm negative space, restrained window glow.
2. `02_small_goal.png`: Same room and same present teenager. Close-medium view as he calmly reaches toward one small unfinished handmade model and a closed notebook on the desk, choosing a modest next step rather than celebrating. Focused expression, a single warm desk lamp, realistic hands and objects, no trophy, no certificate text, no readable labels.
3. `03_quiet_contribution.png`: Same present teenager after school in a realistic small community-library study room, still in the same uniform. He sits beside one younger student and quietly helps them understand an open picture book, pointing gently without dominating. Natural shelves, opaque ceiling, warm window light, only a few background people, no readable book text or signs.
4. `04_future_purpose.png`: The exact same protagonist imagined at age 22 inside a modest neighborhood learning center. Same recognizable eyes, face, and black hair, now wearing the navy casual jacket and gray knit. He arranges simple learning materials beside a sunlit window while listening attentively to a visitor, showing that he has found a clearer purpose through service. Natural indoor architecture, no grand office, no visible text.
5. `05_daily_path.png`: A lyrical but physically realistic wide outdoor scene on the same town's riverside walking path at early morning. The present teenager walks away from camera in school uniform along one continuous path toward soft light; subtle successive wet footprints and repeating railing posts suggest daily progress becoming a single path. No duplicate people, no fantasy portals, stable eye-level composition.
6. `06_small_achievements.png`: The future 22-year-old in a modest work corner at the learning center, same face and future clothes. Around him are small, believable traces of five years of effort: several closed notebooks, a healthy plant, one handmade model, a few unprinted photographs turned partly away, and neatly organized learning materials. He pauses with curious attention over a new blank project, showing both gathered achievements and continued seeking. No medals, trophies, diplomas, or readable text.
7. `07_courage_and_maturity.png`: Same future 22-year-old at the front of a small community workshop with six or seven ordinary participants seated in a natural multipurpose room. He has just chosen to speak, one hand lightly raised, posture composed but not heroic, expression slightly nervous and quietly courageous. Participants are supportive, not cheering. Real ceiling and walls, restrained warm light, no stage spectacle, no readable presentation screen.
8. `08_future_opens.png`: Final quiet hopeful wide shot at gentle morning. Same future 22-year-old opens the simple glass entrance doors of the neighborhood learning center and steps toward the sunlit riverside street, carrying the same brown messenger bag. A few ordinary people approach in the middle distance. The future feels open through depth, air, and soft gold light, not through wealth or grandeur. Keep the subject in upper-middle frame and leave a calm lower area for subtitles. No readable signage.
