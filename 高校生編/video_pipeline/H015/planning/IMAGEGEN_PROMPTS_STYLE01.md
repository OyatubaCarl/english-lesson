# H015 ImageGen prompt set — style 01

## Approved style and production intent

- Use case: illustration-story
- Video: H015「年の瀬の静けさ」採用Suno曲の映像化
- Style: 01「光彩・劇場アニメ背景」
- Music mood: intimate indie-folk and felt-piano ballad, acoustic guitar, cello, light drums, restrained winter bells
- Priority: lyric/time consistency > character continuity > natural temple/home > caption readability > beauty
- Format: 16:9 cinematic 2D theatrical-anime illustration, precise drawn background, hand-painted winter light, cel-painted characters, subtle atmospheric perspective
- Camera: stable eye-level view; no Dutch angle, shake, motion blur, fisheye, or exaggerated wide-angle distortion
- Caption safety: keep primary faces and actions in the upper and middle half; the lower portion will receive a dark subtitle panel
- Prohibited: photorealism, photographic texture, PBR, 3D render, readable text, letters, numbers, logos, signs, captions, borders, watermark

## Continuity anchors

- Present protagonist: the same 17-year-old Japanese male high-school student as H014, short slightly tousled black hair, calm brown eyes, slim natural build, navy school uniform under a dark-navy wool coat, gray scarf, black backpack
- Younger protagonist: same boy at about age 12, recognizable face and black hair, navy sweatshirt and gray trousers
- Younger sister: about age 9, chin-length black hair, calm brown eyes, mustard cardigan and deep-green skirt
- Late grandfather in warm memory only: late sixties, short silver hair, gentle eyes, oatmeal cardigan and gray shirt
- Temple: small neighborhood Buddhist temple with wooden gate, main hall, bonsho bell tower, stone path and bare trees; absolutely no torii or shimenawa
- Color arc: cold indigo year-end dusk in the present; muted amber childhood memory; gentle blue-gold dawn for the new habit; warm soft gold for grandfather memory; quiet indigo and lantern amber at the end

## Scene prompts

1. `01_year_end_temple_approach.png`: Wide establishing shot at winter dusk. The present 17-year-old walks alone up a quiet residential slope toward the wooden gate of a small neighborhood Buddhist temple, seen three-quarter from behind. He wears the dark-navy wool coat over his uniform, gray scarf and black backpack. Bare branches, faint breath in cold air, small warm temple lanterns, deep indigo sky, reflective rather than sad. Bonsho bell tower glimpsed inside, no torii, no shrine rope, no readable signs.
2. `02_last_year_memory.png`: The same temple gate one year earlier in softly desaturated winter light. The same boy at age 16, same recognizable face and black hair, pauses just inside the gate and looks toward the quiet main hall. A thin dusting of old snow remains beside the stone path. Frame the memory with restrained warm-gray light, not a dream cloud or split screen. No text, no torii.
3. `03_present_reflection.png`: Present year, same 17-year-old in the temple courtyard. Medium-wide side view as he stands beside a stone basin and looks down at his loosely clasped hands, calmly examining his own year. The wooden main hall and bare branches recede naturally behind him; blue-gray dusk with one modest amber lantern. Thoughtful face, no melodramatic tears, lower frame kept visually calm.
4. `04_regret_at_steps.png`: Same present teenager seated alone at the edge of broad temple steps, shoulders slightly lowered, remembering words he should not have said. Close-medium profile with his face and hands in upper-middle frame, a few dry leaves crossing the stone, warm light from the main hall touching one cheek against cold indigo air. Show remorse through restrained posture, no written diary, no floating words or symbolic text.
5. `05_younger_brother_snaps.png`: Physically realistic childhood flashback in an ordinary Japanese family living room. The protagonist at age 12, recognizable black hair and face, has just spoken sharply and gestures impatiently toward his nine-year-old sister beside a low table with scattered craft paper. The sister flinches slightly and lowers her eyes; no physical violence, no villainous face. Muted amber evening, real walls and ceiling, no readable paper text.
6. `06_sister_patience.png`: Same childhood room, same 12-year-old brother and nine-year-old sister in the exact same clothes. The sister quietly gathers the scattered craft pieces and offers one back with patient but slightly hurt eyes; the brother's anger has fallen away and he looks ashamed, hand lowered. Intimate medium shot, compassionate and emotionally subtle, not cheerful submission, no text.
7. `07_family_gratitude.png`: A hopeful imagined New Year evening at the same ordinary family dining table. The present 17-year-old, coat removed but still in navy uniform and white shirt, modestly serves tea and places a small dish for his mother, father and younger sister before sitting. Family members respond with quiet warmth, not applause. Natural apartment walls and ceiling, simple meal, amber household light, no holiday signage or readable packaging.
8. `08_small_morning_habit.png`: Gentle blue-gold winter dawn on a realistic riverside path in the same town. The present boy in a plain navy training jacket has risen early and pauses beside the railing, eyes softly closed, breathing deeply with relaxed shoulders as pale sunlight enters the cold air. One faint breath cloud, calm river, distant houses, bare trees. Small sustainable habit, not athletic triumph, no text.
9. `09_bell_and_prayer.png`: Back at the Buddhist temple at indigo night. Same present teenager stands before the main hall with hands pressed together, remembering his late grandfather as the large bonsho bell hangs visibly in the wooden bell tower behind and to one side. A temple attendant has just sounded it with a suspended wooden beam, shown subtly in the background. Warm lanterns, cold air, respectful stillness. Do not depict the grandfather as a ghost; no torii, no shrine bell rope, no text.
10. `10_grandfather_advice.png`: Warm natural memory on a modest Japanese house veranda in late afternoon. The late grandfather in his late sixties, short silver hair and oatmeal cardigan, sits beside the younger protagonist at about age 12. Grandfather has just offered quiet advice with a small gentle look and relaxed hands, speaking few words; the boy listens closely. Garden light, ordinary wood architecture, soft restrained gold, no halo, no ghost effect, no readable objects.
11. `11_inherit_quiet_advice.png`: Final wide shot at the present temple gate after the bell. The same 17-year-old steps back toward the residential street, turning his calm face slightly toward the warm temple light before walking forward. His posture is a little straighter, one hand lightly holding the gray scarf, black backpack on his shoulders. Indigo night, amber lanterns, bare branches and faint breath; quiet resolve rather than celebration. Keep the lower area simple for subtitles, no text, no torii.
